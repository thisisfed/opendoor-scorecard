Method. Each metric scores 0–10 on a straight line between a "0 points" and a "10 points" value (hover a metric to see them). Pillars average their metrics, and the overall score weights the pillars as shown. Grades: A 8+, B 6.5+, C 5+, D 3.5+, F below. Debt is split by what backs it: home-backed debt, which lenders can recover only from the homes, is measured against the homes it funds, and the company's own convertible notes against its cash. Potential new shares count only once they are live at today's price (see Details). A quarterly figure one quarter old is labelled; older than that, it stops counting. The thresholds and weights are judgement calls and have not been back-tested against share-price returns. Q3 revenue is estimated at $1,027M (+12.2% YoY): homes sold × $376.3K average revenue per home, until Opendoor reports. Cash, notes, book value and share count include: $135M of 2026 convertible notes repaid at maturity; $650M 0% convertible notes due 2030, $158M buyback of 45.3M shares, $52.5M capped call.

Sources. SEC EDGAR (financial statements and earnings releases), accountable.opendoor.com, Nasdaq (price), Freddie Mac PMMS (mortgage rate), Realtor.com (housing in Opendoor's markets). Download the score history or all the data.
